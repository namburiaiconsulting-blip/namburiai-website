Case study: strategic cyber-resilience & agentic AI governance

IDCSS 2026, Challenge C, set by Het Security (Netherlands). Winning Team C2, led by our founder. Simulated scenario: Nusantara Bridge Logistics.

A €23,400 fraudulent payment was made by autonomous finance and logistics agents. We consolidated 12 security and governance issues into five root-cause themes and proposed a bounded-autonomy model:

  • Dedicated agent identities, least-privilege access and segregation of duties
  • An authorisation framework with Allow / Review / Block decisions and an auditable decision ledger
  • Risk-based human approval for sensitive actions such as payment beneficiary changes
  • Agent isolation, an independent kill-switch, containment and recovery

Applied: NIST AI Risk Management Framework, NIST Cybersecurity Framework and the EU AI Act.
